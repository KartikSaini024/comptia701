How to use the map

Open a domain, then an objective, and keep going until you reach the cards at the end of each branch: those hold the explanation and exam tips. Tick a card when you know it. Progress is saved in this browser.

Auto-collapse keeps one branch open at a time. Recall blurs every explanation so you can test yourself before tapping to reveal. Next topic walks through every card in syllabus order.
